## FLAGGED (fork kept over upstream, with evidence)

1. **Silent NPC talk** (`hud/dialog.rs`): kept the fork's unconditional `CSStartInteraction` for every selected unit. Evidence: the native dispatch address in the fork comment (`dispatch390f37b3`) and `docs/research/re-housing.md` (`FUN_393befd0` interaction flow). Upstream's local route contradicts this by ending silent talks without the lane.
2. **Chat spam throttle** (`aa-net/src/state/world.rs`, `hud/api.rs` send path): kept the fork's `Chat::allow_send_at` and `ChatSpam` table. Evidence: `FUN_393c2540`, `FUN_391d8cf0`, `FUN_393c5aa0` in `docs/research/re-social.md` (spam config 15 groups + 15 float delays; refusal 0x108). Upstream's `ChatSpamDelay` deliberately does no local throttling and therefore contradicts it. The upstream type/event remains compiled for compatibility but is not emitted by the decoder.
3. **Item-instance tooltip semantics** (`hud/tips.rs`, `hud/panels.rs`): kept the fork's secure/lock state, unlock remain date, `lifeSpan`/`expireTime`, `lookType`/`lookChanged`, crafter and `item_sockets`-derived socket count; upstream's `ItemExtra` supplies gems/rune/dye/procs/scalable. Evidence: `FUN_397cd490`, `FUN_39423560`, `FUN_39421e20`, `FUN_3944d9e0`, `FUN_395aca00`, `FUN_397cc290` and the `tooltip.alb` line ranges in the fork comments (commit `3460c9e`).
4. **Owned mounts / README**: kept the fork's mount/ship/vehicle Transport line and owned-mount flow (`ingame/mount.rs`, `hud/pets/owned.rs`, `mount_anim`; `docs/research/re-mounts.md`). Upstream's "mount work remains deferred" was corrected in the parity checkpoint. This is the documented fork deviation in `docs/aa-client-parity.md`.
5. **Auction opening** (`hud/store.rs`, `hud/services.rs`): the fork's greeting menu still calls `auction::open_service`; upstream's `store/services` route is dormant. This is the documented fork deviation.
