Gaps worth knowing even where the area is covered: the dynamic (per-player) UCC/crest binding behind `CustomTextureManager` is only statically baked in `aa-cry` ("dynamic player crests need a separate binding"); the web buy/password windows (`ViewBuyCashWindow`, `http://%s/store/buy?uuid=%s`, `originPassword...`) have no web backend and are deliberately left out; TSS packet encryption, TQOS reporting and DataMiningManager are anti-cheat/telemetry and are OUT.
